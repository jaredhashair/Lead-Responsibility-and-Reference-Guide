Responsibilities During Shift

  • Assist staff with transports, new admits, bedside procedures, and be available as a clinical resource.
  • Ensure staff clock out and take their breaks prior to the 5-hour mark.
  • Provide patient care, direct and prioritize daily workflows; ensures adequate staffing problem resolutions.
  • Create the next shift work schedule to ensure proper coverage for all patient care areas.
  • Ensure equipment levels are maintained. Clean and set up equipment as needed.
  • Responsible and accountable for providing quality patient care and customer service.
  • Identify any risk management, patient, and/or hospital safety issues and take steps to resolve.

Staff Injury During Shift

Co-workers must go to Occupational Health (if Occupational Health is closed, they must go to the ED). A SQIS is to be filled out with all details of the injury.

Internal Disaster

On notification of Code Triage

  1. Report immediately to your department for further instructions.
  2. Keep phones and pagers clear for disaster-related communication only.
  3. Determine staffing needs — who's needed and who can be spared for other duties.
  4. On-duty staff check in with their department head/manager for updates; off-duty staff contact their supervisor/chair if possible.
  5. Activate the department's staff recall list as needed. Recalled staff enter through the main lobby and must bring their hospital ID badge.

If the disaster involves possible damage to the hospital structure

  1. Account for all staff known to be in your area before the event.
  2. Starting at one end of the area, check every room for injured persons, physical damage, or safety hazards — using safe search practices throughout.
  3. Check for water leaks, fire/smoke, gas odor, structural damage, or other hazards.
  4. Escort injured persons to the Triage Area; notify the Triage Area Coordinator of anyone who can't be moved.
  5. Cordon off any areas felt to be unsafe.
  6. Once all injured have been attended to, begin clean-up.

On "Code Triage: All Clear"

Return to your home department and debrief with your supervisor. Return all deployed equipment and supplies to their designated storage locations as demobilization wraps up.

Equipment Failure on Patient

Remove the equipment from service, tag or label to prevent further use.

  • Don't change the equipment settings.
  • Save any consumable items used with the equipment.
  • Notify your Supervisor.
  • Complete an Incident Report.
  • Report equipment to Biomedical Engineering (714-456-5366).
  • Provide the following information:
    • Location of equipment
    • Equipment type and model number
    • Equipment number (Biomed#)
    • Your name and extension
    • Description of failure/malfunction
